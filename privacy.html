Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

CounterFactual Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the CounterFactual channels (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok) to publish the owner's own videos and read comments on them.

What it accesses

Only the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts: to upload videos the owner created and to read public comments on those videos so the audience's answers can shape the next episode.

What it stores

Video IDs, and the text and public usernames of comments on the owner's videos, kept in a private repository. No other personal data is collected. Nothing is sold or shared.

Third parties

Data is exchanged only with Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram and TikTok through their official APIs.

Removal

Commenters can delete their comment on the platform at any time. To request removal of stored comment data, contact the channel through YouTube.
